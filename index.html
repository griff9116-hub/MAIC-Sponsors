The Process

How It Works: Quarterly Rolling Access

MAIC runs year-round, with new startups joining every quarter. Engage with multiple cohorts simultaneously, so you always have fresh opportunities to explore.

Every Quarter

New Startup Cohort

Review startup profiles, attend innovation briefings, and request 1:1 introductions with the startups most relevant to your priorities.

Q1

Scouting & Alignment

Kickoff workshop, startup pitches, and strategic priority alignment, ensuring every introduction is calibrated to your goals.

Q2

Deep Dives & Pilots

Structured startup deep dives, pilot discussions, and advisory board sessions to move from scouting to serious evaluation.

Q3

Partnership Development

Pilot programme launches, custom demo days, and ROI case studies to build the evidence base for strategic decisions.

Q4

Showcase & Commitments

Innovation showcase, partnership signings, and annual strategic review to set the agenda for the year ahead.
